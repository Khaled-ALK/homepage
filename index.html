About Me

I am a Master's student in Mathematics at Saarland University. My academic journey is driven by a deep fascination with the mathematical foundations of learning and decision-making processes.

Currently, my focus lies at the intersection of optimization, game theory, and machine learning. I am particularly interested in understanding the theoretical guarantees of algorithms operating in uncertain environments.

Research Interests

  • Theoretical Reinforcement Learning: Bandit algorithms, MDPs, value iteration, and exploration strategies.
  • Algorithmic Game Theory: Minimax theorems, no-regret learning, and equilibria in games.
  • Convex Optimization: Linear programming, duality, and their applications in machine learning paradigms.
