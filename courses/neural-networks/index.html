Neural Networks: Theory & Implementation

M.Sc. Mathematics | Saarland University (Winter 2026/2027) - Dietrich Klakow

This course bridges the mathematical theory of deep learning with its practical implementation. Starting from foundational linear algebra and numerical computation, it scales up to advanced architectures including Convolutional Neural Networks, Recurrent Networks, Transformers, and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs).


As a mathematics student, my focus is heavily on the theoretical guarantees, optimization landscapes, and strict mathematical formulations of these models rather than just black-box implementations.

Personal Note & Errata: During my studies, I critically reviewed one of the core textbooks for this course, Deep Learning Architectures: A Mathematical Approach by Ovidiu Calin. I compiled a formal LaTeX errata document correcting several mathematical inaccuracies (e.g., Lemma 4.2.2 on page 76, the assumption of uncountably infinite hidden units in Section 7.3, and typographical errors on page 223). The notes below reflect this level of mathematical rigor.
