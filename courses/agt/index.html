Algorithmic Game Theory

Independent Study based on Tim Roughgarden's Stanford CS364A (Fall 2013).

A broad survey of topics at the interface of theoretical computer science and economics. This course introduces auction and mechanism design with an emphasis on computational efficiency and robustness, and explores the "price of anarchy" with applications to networks.


It covers algorithms and complexity theory for learning and computing Nash and market equilibria. The foundational concepts from this course, such as Myerson's Lemma, VCG mechanisms, and regret minimization, provide crucial intuition for both game theory and multi-agent reinforcement learning.
