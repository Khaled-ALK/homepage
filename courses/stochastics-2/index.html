Stochastics II

M.Sc. Mathematics | Saarland University (Winter 2026/2027) - Henryk Zähle

This course follows on from Stochastics I and provides a rigorous mathematical foundation in advanced probability theory and stochastic processes. Building on measure-theoretic probability, the course explores the structural properties of stochastic models which are essential for advanced machine learning, reinforcement learning, and theoretical computer science.


A core component of the course is the weekly assignments, which require solid proofs and a deep understanding of measure theory. Achieving at least 50% on these assignments is necessary to qualify for the final oral examination.

Topics Covered

Part I – Core Foundations

  • Conditioning on σ-algebras
  • Conditional expectations & distributions
  • Basic concepts of stochastic processes
  • Kolmogorov’s extension theorem

Part II – Advanced Processes

  • Poisson processes
  • Gaussian processes
  • Brownian motion
  • Markov processes
  • Martingales
