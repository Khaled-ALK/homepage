Foundations of Reinforcement Learning

Independent Study & Notes | ECE 524 (Spring 2024) - Chi Jin (Princeton University)

Recently, I've been heavily focused on theoretical reinforcement learning, particularly bandit algorithms, Markov decision processes, and value iteration frameworks. I became curious about the convergence proofs and theoretical guarantees behind UCB, LinUCB, Fitted Q-Iteration, LSVI-UCB, and linear MDPs. To solidify my understanding, I am following this comprehensive course, which brings together concentration inequalities, theoretical RL exploration, and large state space approximations into one coherent framework.

Topics Covered & Schedule

Part I – Tabular MDP (Basics)

  • Intro, MDP basics and planning
  • Concentration inequalities & Martingales
  • Generative models, value iteration
  • Online RL, exploration, optimism
  • Minimax lower bound & Offline RL (pessimism)

Part II – Advanced Topics

  • Offline learning
  • Large state space, linear function approximation
  • General function approximation
  • Game theory and multiagent RL
  • Learning Markov games & POMDPs
